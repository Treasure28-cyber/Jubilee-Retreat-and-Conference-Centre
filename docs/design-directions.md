# Four distinct hospitality directions

These are visual proposals, not claims about services or facilities. Jubilee is implemented in this repository; the other three are directions for separate projects, subject to their own briefs and approved assets.

| Property | Hero layout | Type pairing | Signature | Section rhythm | Logo brief |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Jubilee | Centered pool photograph; enquiry card crosses the lower seam on desktop and follows the hero on mobile | Cormorant Garamond + Source Sans 3 | Slim gold rules with section numbers | Formal opening, practical enquiry, trust strip, paired service photography, events feature, Google rating, direct booking, location | Typographic wordmark with a thin arch and small cross; navy and gold; no initial-letter badge |
| Carlaw | An editorial split: oversized place-led headline, tall architectural photograph, concise booking bar below | DM Sans + Lora | Narrow vertical photo captions and a warm terracotta underline | Building, rooms, dining, neighbourhood, direct contact; alternate full-width images and short text passages | Custom letter spacing and a small architectural bracket derived from the actual facade; avoid a generic monogram |
| Tozak | Pool photography with left-aligned welcoming copy and a compact room request beside it | Fraunces + Manrope | A softly curved pool-edge divider and deep teal accents | Arrival, rooms and price choices, pool, food, directions; use short practical sections | A restrained wave integrated into a distinctive wordmark; one-colour version must work at small sizes |
| Victory View | A panoramic image above a broad booking bar; headline sits in a separate solid-colour panel | Archivo + Newsreader | Large cropped image windows and bold room-category numerals | First orient visitors, then rooms, dining, gatherings, access and contact; use a stronger image/text alternation | A horizon cut through a custom wordmark; avoid a generic V emblem and keep any view motif grounded in the actual property |

Keep prices, amenities, reviews and capacities tied to verified source material for each property. Reuse accessible interaction patterns where useful; do not reuse the same hero composition, visual motif or page sequence across all four.
